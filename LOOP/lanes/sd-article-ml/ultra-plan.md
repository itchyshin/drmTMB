## 5. Article specification

Reader: an applied ecologist who can fit a random-intercept model but has not used a model for its SD.

Working title: retain "When group variation changes with a predictor". Explain the ecological question before the equations, then log s_g = alpha_0 + alpha_1 z_g and b_g ~ Normal(0,s_g^2). For a structured field, Cov(b)=D K D, so s_g is a multiplier and marginal SD is s_g sqrt(K_gg). A unit increase in z multiplies SD by exp(alpha_1), variance by exp(2 alpha_1). Do not confuse raw-response spread, residual sigma and latent-effect spread.

Sections: (1) question and three kinds of spread; (2) existing Bernoulli/Poisson example, explicitly without a free residual sigma; (3) short Gaussian comparison linked to the existing LSS tutorial; (4) one small Gamma related-matrix example with a non-unit diagonal and continuous group predictor, admitted only after the exact route passes; (5) matched R/native, R/Julia and direct-Julia snippets naming their distinct public interfaces; (6) conditional stored predictions versus fresh RE=0 predictions; (7) generated capability table and troubleshooting; (8) measured limits and deferred interval calibration.
