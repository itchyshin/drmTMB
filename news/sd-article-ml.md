* Julia bridge `predict()` now uses retained structured conditional fields for stored `mu` predictions on selected Gaussian, Poisson, NB2, Gamma and Binomial SD-regression routes. Explicit `newdata` predictions still set random effects to zero. Binomial response predictions use the fitted link. Structured conditional fitted values avoid inversion of rounded probabilities or overflowing population means, and their response residuals use the retained response to avoid cancellation.
* The random-SD article adds a continuous-predictor Gamma example with a raw relatedness matrix. It separates the latent SD multiplier, marginal group-effect SD and conditional response spread, and keeps its single-draw estimation error visible. These examples do not establish interval calibration or general engine parity.
