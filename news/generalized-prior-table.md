* Extend native `drmTMB()` prior tables with normalized Student-t coefficient priors, Gamma priors on supported natural residual SDs, and LKJ priors for bivariate residual correlation or one supported Gaussian intercept/slope correlation. `drm_resolve_priors()` previews resolved targets. The Julia bridge now has versioned contracts for selected residual-SD and correlation combinations; unsupported combinations remain refused.
