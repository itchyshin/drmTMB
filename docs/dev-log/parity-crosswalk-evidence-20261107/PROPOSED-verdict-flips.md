### Honest findings that must travel with any flip

1. **Gamma crossed joint mu + sigma (row 18) is outer bimodality, not a defect.** `wave4-gamma-crossed-diagnosis`: an independent dense Laplace oracle gives -832.50851620 at Julia's estimate and -832.52452715 at R's; the inner random-effect mode is unique at both. Thirty random starts per side land on exactly two optima: Julia 14 at R's, 16 at Julia's; R (`nlminb` on the drmTMB objective) 25 at its own, 5 at Julia's. Julia's default start finds the higher optimum (by 0.016); R's default finds the lower. The straight line between them dips about 0.7 in log-likelihood. Size of the difference: log-lik gap 0.016 (2 x gap = 0.03); the sd(id) slope differs by 1.9 SE; R lands on the lower optimum in 25 of 30 random starts. That is not "statistically indistinguishable": the SD estimates differ and the gap is small, not nil. One dataset, one start distribution; it does not show R is generally worse.
