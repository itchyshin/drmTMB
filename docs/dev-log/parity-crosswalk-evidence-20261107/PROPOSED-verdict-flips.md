### Honest findings that must travel with any flip

1. **Gamma crossed joint mu + sigma (row 18) is outer bimodality, not a defect.** `wave4-gamma-crossed-diagnosis`: an independent dense Laplace oracle gives -832.50851620 at Julia's estimate and -832.52452715 at R's; the inner random-effect mode is unique at both. Thirty random starts per side land on exactly two optima: Julia 14 at R's, 16 at Julia's; R (`nlminb` on the drmTMB objective) 25 at its own, 5 at Julia's. Julia's default start finds the higher optimum (by 0.016); R's default finds the lower. The straight line between them dips about 0.7 in log-likelihood. 0.016 is far below any test-statistic scale (about 0.03 on 2 x log-lik). One dataset, one start distribution; it does not show R is generally worse. Suggested wording for the matrix: agrees at the shared stationary point (R's objective at Julia's estimate equals Julia's to 3e-9); default fits land on different maxima of a bimodal surface; Julia's is the higher.
2. **Poisson spatial q2 is the weakest cell.** `scale300`, 300 seeds: 7 seeds beyond 1e-3 R-SE units and 9 with log-lik difference above 1e-6. Diagnosed with R's objective at Julia's estimate and a 40-start R oracle: seeds 88, 91, 204, 211 and 20 are R stopping at a lower local maximum (R lower by 0.17 to 0.40, and by 1.3e-3 for seed 20, whose Hessian is not positive definite); seed 147 is Julia stopping at a lower maximum (1.02 log-lik below R's); seed 118 is boundary non-identification. 5 of 300 are R stopping short and 1 is Julia. In `multiseed`, seed 9 shows the slope-SD coefficient at the boundary (Julia -14.49 and -0.448, R -11.55 and -0.148, log-lik diff 1.0e-8, R SE about 5000, Julia Inf): the likelihood is flat there and the coefficient is not identified. `multiseed2` ran ten start vectors per side on that seed: log-likelihood equal to 3e-10 on 9 of 10 starts, one Julia stop 3.4e-5 lower on the ridge.
3. **Wave 5 fix for Poisson spatial seed 147.** Julia now optimises from a second start (SD coefficients at log 0.7) and keeps it only if the objective improves by more than 1e-6. Over the 300 campaign datasets 299 are bit-identical and seed 147 now matches R (log-lik diff -4e-10). It is a heuristic (a third mode could exist), doubles fit time on this route, and was not applied to NB2 spatial or the other structured routes.
4. **Labelled spatial Gaussian seed 2 sits at the rho bound** (`multiseed2`). Julia `eta_cor` 19.15, R 11.59, both rho = 0.999999 (difference 1.7e-10). A dense 1024 x 1024 oracle with no package code gives the same likelihood at both estimates (-824.685736753 and -824.685736756) and a profile flat to 1e-9 beyond eta about 11. R SE about 7600, Julia Inf with a singular-Hessian warning. Same likelihood, same boundary; not a different maximum. The other nine seeds agree to 1.8e-9 R-SE units.
5. **Boundary cases elsewhere in `scale300`.** Labelled ordinary Poisson and NB2: 15 of 600 fits have rho at the boundary; the 13 `lord_nb2` and 1 `lord_poisson` log-lik differences above 1e-6 are all rho-boundary fits where Julia's value is 1e-6 to 6e-6 below R's (flat ridge, tolerance), two of them Julia non-converged flags (seeds 232 and 276). Also nb2_relmat seed 133, poisson_phylo seed 54 and poisson_spatial seeds 157, 279, 282 sit at a slope-SD or intercept-SD boundary, with large absolute coefficient and relative SD differences for a quantity that is about zero (up to 2.5e3 relative) and log-lik differences under about 2e-6. NB2 structured sigma phylo and spatial seed 2 (`wave4-structured-sigma`) are the same boundary kind: log-lik differences 5.2e-9 and 6.6e-9, slope SD at log -11.6 in R and about -16 in Julia.
6. **Gaussian ordinary line-search failure and its fix.** `scale300`: Julia threw `AssertionError: isfinite(phi_c) && isfinite(dphi_c)` on 36 of 300 Gaussian ordinary seeds (Julia 1.10.12, Totoro) and 26 of 300 on Julia 1.12.7 (different datasets); R converged on all of them; the other 264 agreed to 9.8e-10 R-SE units. Root cause (`wave5-ordinary-robust`): the first L-BFGS step is the raw gradient (length of order 1e2 to 1e3), the first probe lands at log-sigma about -770, the information matrix overflows, and on a later probe it is finite but of order 1e170 so Cholesky "succeeds" and the objective or its AD gradient is NaN. Whether a dataset hits the sentinel branch or the NaN branch depends on floating-point detail, so the failing set differs by platform and Julia version. Fix: `_optimize_with_fallback` now returns the 1e18 sentinel for any non-finite probe inside the optimiser and retries with BackTracking if HagerZhang still asserts. After the fix, 0 failures and 300 of 300 converged in all four configurations (Julia 1.10.12 and 1.12.7, each on its own and the other's datasets; 1,200 fits), max coefficient difference 9.8e-10 R-SE units, max rho difference 6.6e-9, max log-lik difference 2.1e-10. The 1,064 fits that already succeeded are bit-identical before and after. The same guard was applied to the Gaussian structured animal/relmat q2 fitter (300 seeds, 600 fits, 0 failures before and after, bit-identical). q3 and q4 share the ordinary fitter, so they get the guard, but no 300-seed run was made for them.
7. **Gaussian REML relmat seed 1, ML (not REML): Julia higher by 3.57** (R -402.4469, Julia -398.8762, both converged). Same kind of outer bimodality as item 1; the REML fit of the same dataset agrees to 1e-10. 29 of 30 ML reference cells agree. Not diagnosed further.

### REML scope (`wave4-reml`)

REML is shown only on shapes native R accepts under `REML = TRUE`: unlabelled `animal`/`relmat`/`spatial(1 + x | g)` with `sigma ~ 1`, `phylo(1 + x | g)` unlabelled with `sigma ~ 1` and with `sigma ~ x`, and labelled `phylo(1 + x | p | g)`. R refuses ordinary q2/q3/q4 and joint mu+sigma with `sd()` formulas, labelled animal/relmat/spatial, and animal/relmat/spatial with `sigma ~ x`; Julia refuses the same shapes (agreement of refusals, not of fits). Julia's REML was first implemented with `beta_mu` as a free coordinate and stopped 5.13 log-lik units below R's optimum on relmat seed 4; profiling `beta_mu` by GLS (as TMB does once `beta_mu` is integrated) fixed it. Dense n x n marginal, so n at most 5000. REML structured sigma with SD formula is not implemented. Mean-coefficient Wald intervals under REML were not examined. No REML verdict beyond these shapes should be written.

### Post-fit (`wave4-postfit`)

Conditional quantile residuals: native `residuals(type = "quantile")` for fitted rows is conditional on the Laplace modes (item 9). Julia used the population level, so its residuals were over-dispersed (SD 1.07 to 3.27 on test fixtures). Julia now stores the per-row link-scale mode contribution and uses it. Ten fits (labelled animal Poisson, NB2, Gamma, Gaussian from `A` and `Ainv`, plus ordinary unlabelled Poisson and NB2): log-lik within 6.0e-10, conditional mean relative 2.2e-7, sigma 2.0e-8, PIT bounds 8.2e-8, deterministic residuals (Gamma, Gaussian) 1.6e-7, and every count row's Julia `Phi(r)` lies in R's interval `[lo, hi]` (all six count fits). Not compared with R: phylo/spatial q2, two-field crossed and relmat shapes, Gaussian q3/q4, joint mu+sigma (dense-computation checks in Julia only, 1e-8). R does not fit Gamma ordinary correlated or structured sigma with an SD formula, so no R comparator exists. No matrix row has a post-fit column; nothing to flip.
