Status (2026-09-14, second audit arc, branch `claude/audit-dinnage-wave1-20260913`, draft PR #1361).

**The eleven remaining sites are fixed** (`fc461aae4`): every `mi_family` 2–12 quadrature block (ordinal, categorical, beta, zero-one-beta, beta-binomial, Poisson, NB2, truncated NB2, lognormal, gamma, Tweedie) now applies `weights(i)` to the combined mixture log-density and to the observed-row imputation prior, matching the Bernoulli template. The new per-family test (`tests/testthat/test-dinnage-audit-m1-families.R`) asserts, at converged optima, the exact identity that the `weights = 2` objective equals twice the `weights = 1` objective, plus coefficient equality and literal row duplication. On the previous kernel it fails 43 times across all eleven families; after the fix, 0.

Two neighbours found while doing this, recorded rather than fixed:

1. **The Gaussian latent `mi()` route (`mi_family == 0`) also breaks weight invariance**, by a different mechanism: its covariate-model density inside the Laplace-integrated latent carries no weight (max coefficient shift 0.013 at `weights = 2` on the probe). Weighting both terms inside a Laplace-integrated latent is not the same as weighting the marginal, so this needs its own design rather than a one-line change.
2. **The Tweedie imputation model sizes its fixed 35-node Legendre support from `stats::var()` of the observed covariate**, which divides by n − 1, so duplicating rows moves the support and the same integral evaluates about 1.3 nats apart at identical parameters. The weight algebra is exact (the objective identity holds to 1e-10); the duplication arm of the Tweedie test is loosened to 1e-2 for this reason, with the mechanism named in the file.

Leaving the issue open for item 1; item 2 is a separate hygiene finding (quadrature support should not depend on a moment estimate).
