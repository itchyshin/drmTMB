TITLE: [Dinnage audit A-7] Nothing on the diagnostic board carries sample size; the board gets cleaner as n falls
LABELS: audit-dinnage, enhancement, moderate
---
Reported by Russell Dinnage, independent evaluation of drmTMB 0.7.0 at 945da24f
(https://github.com/rdinnager/drmTMB_eval/blob/main/REPORT.md §4, Appendix A row A-7; severity moderate).
Supporting working: pure noise with a six-parameter scale submodel at n = 60 vs n = 200.

## What Russell found
At n = 60, 99.8-100% of fits are fully green against 95.2-96.2% at n = 200; `is_converged()` is TRUE in
2000 of 2000; neither drmTMB nor its comparator warns. Meanwhile 58.8% (MCSE 1.1%) of pure-noise datasets
yield at least one significant term and 35.9% a significant *scale* term at the small n. The type-I
inflation itself is a shared parity issue (§6), but the observation here is narrower: the diagnostic board
gets *cleaner*, not more cautious, exactly where inference is worst, because nothing on it is scaled by
observations-per-estimated-parameter.

## Where (at 945da24f)
`R/check.R`.

## Since-audit status on main (4bfb9d721, 2026-09-13)
PRESENT. Name-scanning every `check_*` function currently in `R/check.R` (~34 rows: convergence, gradient,
Hessian, SE finiteness/inflation, dropped rows, clamp, boundary values, phylo/spatial diagnostics, family-
specific `nu` checks) finds none that reference `nobs()`, the number of estimated parameters, or their
ratio. `check_fixed_effect_design_size()` (`R/check.R:1566`) checks the design matrix's column count
against row count for rank/estimability, which is a related but distinct concern (singularity, not
small-sample inflation risk on an otherwise well-posed fit). `git log --oneline 945da24f..HEAD --
R/check.R` shows no commit adding an observations-per-parameter row. No existing-issue match: `gh issue
list --search "sample size"` and `--search "check_drm"` return capability-parity and existing-row issues,
none proposing this row.

## Proposed fix (Russell's, if stated)
"An observations-per-estimated-parameter row. Sample size is the only signal available in that regime and
nothing on the board carries it."
