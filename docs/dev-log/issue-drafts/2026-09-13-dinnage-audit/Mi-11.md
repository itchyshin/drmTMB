TITLE: [Dinnage audit Mi-11] `?student` claims Student-t is "the one implemented family" whose `sigma` is a scale rather than `SD[y]`; false, and contradicts `?sigma.drmTMB`
LABELS: audit-dinnage, documentation, minor
---
Reported by Russell Dinnage, independent evaluation of drmTMB 0.7.0 at 945da24f
(https://github.com/rdinnager/drmTMB_eval/blob/main/REPORT.md §4, Appendix A row Mi-11; severity minor).
Supporting working: cross-read against `?sigma.drmTMB` and the family list.

## What Russell found
`?student` asserts Student-t is the only implemented family whose public `sigma` is a scale rather than
`SD[y]`. False: gamma, nbinom2, beta, beta_binomial and Tweedie are none of them `SD[y]` either.
`?sigma.drmTMB` is right and `?student` contradicts it -- and `?student` is exactly the page a worried
user reads.

## Where (at 945da24f)
`man/student.Rd`.

## Since-audit status on main (4bfb9d721, 2026-09-13)
PRESENT. `man/student.Rd`'s Details section states verbatim: "This is the one implemented family whose
public `sigma` is a scale rather than `SD[y]`: the location-scale t has no closed-form standard-deviation
parameterization, and both `drmTMB` and its `DRM.jl` twin fit `sigma` as the scale." `R/methods.R:4035`
(`sigma.drmTMB`) treats `gaussian`, `student`, `skew_normal`, `lognormal`, `gamma`, `tweedie`, `beta`,
`zero_one_beta`, `beta_binomial`, `nbinom2`, `truncated_nbinom2`, `hurdle_nbinom2`, `zi_nbinom2` uniformly
via `predict(object, dpar = "sigma")` with no distinguishing SD-vs-scale semantics documented per family
at that call site either, so the "one implemented family" claim in `?student` is unqualified and, per
Russell's point, incorrect for at least gamma, nbinom2, beta, beta_binomial and Tweedie. `git log --oneline
945da24f..HEAD -- man/student.Rd` shows no correcting commit. No existing-issue match: #1230 (a known
overlap, family-doc contradiction about which REs are fitted) is a different contradiction on a different
page; `gh issue list --search "student.Rd"` returns nothing else on point.

## Proposed fix (Russell's, if stated)
"One sentence" -- remove or qualify the "one implemented family" claim in `?student`.
