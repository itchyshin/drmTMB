TITLE: [Dinnage audit S6] Percentile bootstrap confint() doubles the ML bias and under-covers, one-sidedly
LABELS: audit-dinnage, bug, major
---
Reported by Russell Dinnage, independent evaluation of drmTMB 0.7.0 at 945da24f
(https://github.com/rdinnager/drmTMB_eval/blob/main/REPORT.md §4, Appendix A row S6; severity major).
Supporting working: gamma0 = -0.4 (`sigma` intercept), n = 100, R = 199 (package default), Wald/profile/
bootstrap intervals computed on the same fits, 250 replicates, reproduced on two further seeds.

## What Russell found
On the (mildly biased) `sigma` intercept, the percentile bootstrap's midpoint offset from the ML
estimate is 2.35x the ML bias itself (offset -0.05479 vs bias -0.02330), and its coverage is 0.852
against Wald's 0.896 and profile's 0.912. The loss is one-sided: it misses high on 14.5-14.8% of
replicates and low on 0.0-1.0%, the signature of a percentile interval built from an already-shrunk
fit. On an unbiased coefficient (`mu:x1`) there is no such gap (bootstrap 0.928/0.895 vs Wald
0.952/0.900). Convergence was 100% with no dropped replicates, so the finding is "the percentile
bootstrap inherits this estimator's bias twice," not "the bootstrap is bad at n=100" in general.

## Where (at 945da24f)
`R/profile.R:2545` (`drm_bootstrap_confint`), reached from `R/profile.R:501`.

## Since-audit status on main (4bfb9d721, 2026-09-13)
PRESENT. `drm_bootstrap_confint()` is now at `R/profile.R:2608`, reached from the `method = "bootstrap"`
branch of `confint.drmTMB` at `R/profile.R:507-513`. The implementation (`R/profile.R:2608-2760+`) is an
ordinary parametric percentile bootstrap: `stats::simulate(object, nsim = R, seed = seed, re.form =
re_form)` draws from the fitted model, each draw is refit (`bootstrap_refit_one`), and the interval is
`bootstrap_percentile_interval()` (line 2978) — quantiles of the refit draws at `probs = c((1-level)/2,
(1+level)/2)` (line 2679) with no bias-correction (no BCa, no bias-correction term anywhere in this
function or its helpers `bootstrap_percentile_draws`/`bootstrap_uses_link_percentiles`,
lines 2978-3010+). `git log --oneline 945da24f..HEAD -- R/profile.R` shows 10 commits touching this
file; the two mentioning "bootstrap" (`fe7e4ed50`, `6a4a05894`) fix mask-preserving and
`cbind(successes, failures)` reconstruction in bootstrap *refits*, not the percentile-interval
construction or any bias correction — the plain-percentile mechanism itself is unchanged. No
existing-issue match found (checked "bootstrap confint", "percentile" in open-issues.json).

## Proposed fix (Russell's, if stated)
"Use Wald or profile on the scale intercept — they are 4-9 points better on the identical fits — or
ship a bias-corrected variant" (e.g. bias-corrected-and-accelerated, BCa).
