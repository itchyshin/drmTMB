Status (2026-09-15). `drmTMB()` (`R/drmTMB.R`) now drops unused levels from factor columns before model-frame construction, so an unused factor level no longer zeroes out every fixed-effect standard error (`36f903a60`). A follow-up (`743024b8b`) narrowed the scope after local `R CMD check` found it had also re-levelled ordinal responses and `mi()` predictors: `drm_droplevels_fixed_predictors()` now drops unused levels only from factor columns in plain fixed-effect terms, and responses, random-bar groups, and `mi()`/`phylo()`/`relmat()`/`spatial()`/`meta_V()`/`precision()` arguments keep their declared level sets. `tests/testthat/test-dinnage-audit-wave1.R` adds the Md-E block (a 3-level factor with one unused level, all four fixed-effect SEs finite after the fix), and `tests/testthat/test-julia-formula-constructs.R` was updated to assert the TMB fit no longer carries the unused-level coefficient. Merged into `main` via PR #1361 (`8195b1215`); NEWS.md, "Independent-evaluation fixes (wave 1)". Credit: Russell Dinnage (rdinnager/drmTMB_eval), finding Md-E. Closing.
