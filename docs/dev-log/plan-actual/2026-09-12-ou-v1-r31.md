# Plan versus actual — OU v1 R3.1 exact marginal diagnostic

## Approved scope

R3.1 was approved to compare native Laplace and direct small-tree stationary
OU marginal objectives at one interior and one high-rate/low-amplitude point.
It did not authorize a recovery campaign, a capability expansion, or R3.2.

## What ran

The new isolated worktree began from R3 commit `5e299e0b0` with a scoped lane
lease. The two-point runner fixed both alpha values and phylogenetic SDs,
optimized the two intercepts natively, and calculated six-dimensional product
Gauss--Hermite objectives at orders 5, 7, and 9. The full local run took 28.1
seconds. The output and checksums are in `docs/dev-log/evidence/ou-v1-r31/`.

## Results and reconciliation

The planned 5/7 comparison was strengthened to 5/7/9 after the interior
5-to-7 refinement (0.0316) showed that seven nodes alone were not enough. The
9-node results retain a material Laplace-versus-direct difference at both
points: 0.4065 NLL units in the interior case and 0.0735 in the ridge case.
The 7-to-9 refinements are 0.0220 and 0.000136 respectively. Fisher reviewed
the result independently and keeps G15 at no-go.

## Exception and handoff

The Unlazy ledger was created after implementation commit `ad53df166`, so
R31-G0 is an audited late-ledger exception, not a clean pre-edit gate. All
other R3.1 checks pass at their explicitly limited scope. The only proposed
next task is R3.2: an approved exact relative-profile comparison. It must
start in a fresh worktree with a prewritten ledger and lease before editing.
