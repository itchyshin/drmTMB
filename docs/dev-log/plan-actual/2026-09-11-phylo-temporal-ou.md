# Plan versus actual: phylogenetic stable intercept plus independent OU

The plan proposed a paired phylogenetic stable intercept and independent temporal OU process, then a profile-calibration campaign before any inference claim. The implementation met its parser, dense-likelihood, reduction, method, simulation, and fixed-mean profile gates. The four-array Rorqual campaign completed 3,500 tasks and was mirrored to Totoro.

Actual evidence did not support the planned calibration promotion. The original 24-fixture point-recovery gate G9 missed its fixed-effect threshold. The approved alternative G9b recovery supported a five-seed timing pilot only. The retained G13 campaign then qualified six of nine primary profile rows, but failed the predeclared intercept coverage criterion in P1--P3 (0.870, 0.922, and 0.895). The user therefore amended the programme to interval feasibility, retaining G13 as a red gate.

Closeout strengthens evidence handling rather than changing that verdict: G13 now binds archive SHA-256 checks and campaign provenance to source `384048d7eb6be3950dcf28a4aef91a3fb616184a`; `check_drm()` and reader documentation distinguish a calculable fit-level profile from inference-ready uncertainty. No refits or new remote campaign were launched.

The next action is a separate phylogenetic OU covariance arc, with its own formula, likelihood, recovery design, and gates. It must not borrow calibration from this additive paired model.
