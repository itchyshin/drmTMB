# Arc 7B plan-versus-actual reconciliation

## Delivered

The contract, symbolic covariance map, deterministic L/LS/LSS/LSSS/DH DGP,
nested effect replication, direct oracle, `metafor` comparator, formula/output
tests, all-attempt reducer, local sentinel, comparator memo, Fisher/Rose
reviews, check log, after-task report, and handover were completed.

## Deliberately not delivered

No DRAC launcher submission, 1,200-replicate campaign, bootstrap engineering
run, status promotion, or PR merge occurred. The meta-analysis vignette gained
an experimental syntax-and-warning section, not a capability claim. The plan
made campaign expansion conditional on local interval feasibility. The dense
LSS sentinel has two incomplete direct-SD profiles, so proceeding would
contradict the pre-registered stop rule.

## Deviations and reasons

DH was initially described alongside the additive Gaussian oracle. Fisher
identified the error: random log-SD creates a scale mixture, not an additive
Gaussian covariance. The contract now fences DH to fitting/extraction smoke
evidence. The first effect DGP was also corrected from unique labels to truly
nested, replicated effects after review.

## Recommendation

Treat Arc 7B as a completed local-contract and negative-feasibility slice. A
new maintainer decision is required before further interval engineering or
remote compute; it should not be silently continued as this arc.
