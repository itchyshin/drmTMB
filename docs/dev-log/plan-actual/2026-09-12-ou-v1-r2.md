# OU v1 R2 information preflight — checkpoint

## Status

## Design and execution

The committed design used two ordered rate pairs, five independent
tree-plus-field draws per pair, 128 species, 12 observations per species, and
three fixed starts: 10 logical data sets and 30 retained fits. The three-start
largest-cell timing smoke projected 191.61 seconds, below the 30-minute local
limit. All 30 fits were therefore run locally.

`Rscript tools/verify-ou-v1-r2.R --local` and `--decision` pass. The clean
source/provenance receipt is `docs/dev-log/evidence/ou-v1-r2/`.

## Result

All 10 logical data sets produced a numerically qualified selected fit, but
the median absolute log-rate error was 0.875 for `alpha_mu` and 1.272 for
`alpha_sigma`; maxima were 2.254 and 3.315. This is worse than the proposed
future recovery screen `log(1.5) ≈ 0.405`.

Fisher's independent review found no simulator–fitter mismatch: the
stationary tip covariance used to generate data is the fitted rooted-OU
marginal covariance, both generated fields enter the fitted components, and
the clamp is not implicated. The pattern is consistent with an
alpha–amplitude likelihood ridge: fitted rates are systematically high while
field SD estimates fall.
