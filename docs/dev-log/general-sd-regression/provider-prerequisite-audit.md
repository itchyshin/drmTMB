## Block and endpoint-member prerequisites

The 148 block-rule rows are an input grammar for the candidate denominator, not a status audit. Native source-shaped endpoint tuples and q domains are detailed in block-dimension-contract.md, “Native ordinary block rules” and “Native structured and process rules”; Julia's actual q1, ordinary q2, structured q1, bivariate q2/q4, and temporal shapes are in its “Julia rules at the pinned dispatch” section. Those spans show engine differences: native ordinary multi-slope and labelled q rules are broader than the Julia Gaussian ordinary q2 route; Julia bivariate q4 is a fixed four-axis intercept partition, not a slope-expanded q8/q12 shape.

The generated ledger adds 6,750 finite cross-endpoint member-set candidates beyond source-shaped tuples. They remain unresolved; no source or mathematical review here justifies promoting arbitrary subsets involving nu, zi, hu, zoi, coi, or rho12 to supported covariance blocks. The 90 structural exclusions apply only to second-endpoint blocks in single-endpoint families. The remaining candidate sets must be reviewed for family-specific formula coherence, valid endpoint links, and parameterization/identifiability. The 1,440 Julia tagged-q4 shapes mirrored into native remain explicit missing-plumbing rows; the native mirror is a denominator obligation, not an admitted native layout.
