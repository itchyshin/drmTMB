## Target contract (C1a)

For the exact formula, define on the NB2 log-mean scale

\[
\eta_i = X_i\beta_\mu + b_{0,s(i)} + x_i b_{1,s(i)},\qquad
\log \sigma_i = X_i^\sigma\beta_\sigma,
\]

with tree precision \(Q\), \(\tau_j=\exp(\ell_j)\), and

\[
\begin{pmatrix}b_0\\b_1\end{pmatrix} \sim
N\left(0,
\begin{bmatrix}
\tau_0^2 & \rho\tau_0\tau_1\\
\rho\tau_0\tau_1 & \tau_1^2
\end{bmatrix}\otimes Q^{-1}\right),
\qquad \rho=0.999999\tanh(\eta_\rho).
\]

The only C1 estimands are `tau_0`, `tau_1`, and the latent phylogenetic
intercept--slope `rho`. They are not `rho12`, observed correlation,
`corpair()` regression, NB2 dispersion, a direct-SD target, or an interval
target. The q2 penalty must include its determinant normalization and the
cross-quadratic term, have a live `eta_rho`, and reduce exactly to the present
independent q-vector penalty at `rho = 0`.
