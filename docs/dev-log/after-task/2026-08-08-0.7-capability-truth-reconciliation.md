# After Task: Reconcile the drmTMB 0.7 capability truth

## Goal

Make the runtime, canonical capability ledger, generated surfaces, reader
documentation, and release blocker report agree on the exact earned binomial
O2 REML surface and the internal-only cumulative-logit O3 evidence, without
starting release, platform, D-43, or compute work.

## Implemented

`drm_validate_reml_spec()` now rejects fixed-effect-only binomial REML before
TMB object construction and directs the user either to add one admitted
ordinary `mu` random effect or fit with `REML = FALSE`. The public binomial REML
surface remains exactly an ordinary unlabelled random intercept or one
independent random slope. Correlated, labelled, structured, and missing-response
variants remain rejected.

The canonical ledger reconciles `mc-0058`, `mc-0060`, `mc-0062`, `mc-0068`, and
`mc-0227`. The two public O2 cells are `diagnostic_only`; fixed-only and
structured cells are rejected by design; the public cumulative-logit slope row
retains ML-Laplace `point_fit_recovery`, while its O3 evidence is internal and
confers no public reporting permission.

## Mathematical Contract

For admitted binomial models, O2 uses the existing joint-Laplace fixed-effect
fold. This task does not introduce a new likelihood, quadrature rule, or
estimand. Its comparator is deliberately weakly independent:
`glmmTMB(REML = TRUE)` exposes the same joint-Laplace construction. Therefore
the deterministic agreement checks implementation parity, not general
non-Gaussian REML validity, bias reduction, recovery, interval calibration, or
coverage.

O3 remains the existing package-private nested AGHQ25 plus Cox--Reid profile
study for one cumulative-logit fixture. It is not reachable through
`drmTMB()` and cannot upgrade a public ML row to an interval or reporting tier.

## Team Learning

Capability truth needs three separate assertions: the runtime gate, the public
evidence tier, and the callable estimator route. A technically completed
internal estimator study cannot upgrade a public row when users cannot request
that estimator. Likewise, deterministic parity with a similar implementation
earns a diagnostic tier, not a recovery or inference claim.

## Known Limitations

Binomial O2 REML has no multi-seed recovery, calibrated intervals, or coverage
evidence. It does not cover fixed-only models, correlated or labelled blocks,
structured providers, missing responses, scale/shape random effects, or other
non-Gaussian families. O3 remains internal and non-reportable. No release
artifact, platform result, D-43 verdict, or CRAN status is produced here.
