# After Task: B4-CI C1–C4 pin drift, and its resolution

Two sessions on 2026-08-03. **Part 1** records the finding as it surfaced during the Arc 5/6
merge, and why that lane deliberately did not fix it. **Part 2** records the fix, done as a
separate task on branch `claude/b4-ci-mc0207-rebaseline`.

---

# Part 1 — The finding (Arc 7a lane, #907)

## Goal

Not a new feature. This is a finding, surfaced while merging Arc 5 (#907) and Arc 6 (#908)
onto current `main`: an attempt to wire the five orphaned `tools/tests/test_*.py` files into
CI (alongside the twelve model-surface promotions those PRs land) uncovered that four of the
five already fail, independent of either PR.

## What Was Found

`tools/integrate_b4_ci_c1.py` through `tools/integrate_b4_ci_c4.py` each fail their
`--check` / `--check-current` mode on unmodified `origin/main`
(`ca46559f3fe020c8e1e363b02d7a9306b65d2c33`), verified in a clean, detached
`git worktree add --detach <tmp> origin/main`, with nothing from Arc 5/6 present:

```
$ python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c1.py --check-current
B4-CI C1: a hard-excluded C1 neighbour changed from the frozen base

$ python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c2.py --check
B4-CI C2: C1, B3, or excluded base-row drift

$ python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c3.py --check-with-later-cohorts
B4-CI C3: C3 cell source or claim-boundary drift

$ python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c4.py --check
B4-CI C4: protected base-row drift
```

## Root Cause

All four scripts define the same `EXCLUDED_IDS = {"mc-0182", "mc-0183", "mc-0207",
"mc-0269"}` — cells considered but deliberately excluded from their respective promotion
cohorts — and all four assert those rows have not changed since the cohort was integrated.
PR #905 (Arc 4b, `a55ddaef8`) split `mc-0207` into three per-q leaves
(`mc-0207`/`mc-0715`/`mc-0716`) and demoted it from `point_fit_recovery` to
`evidence_tier=none`, with its own review (Noether/Fisher/Rose) and its own
capability-ledger invariants (`FROZEN_CENSUS_POINT_FIT_RECOVERY`) satisfied. But nothing
wired these four B4-CI scripts into CI at any point (see
`docs/dev-log/after-task/2026-08-01-b4-ci-c1-rebase-canonical-integration.md` through
`2026-08-02-b4-ci-c4-canonical-integration.md`: each was run once, by hand, at its own
integration time, and never added to `.github/workflows/R-CMD-check.yaml`), so nobody —
human or CI — checked whether the Arc 4b split was compatible with what these four guards
protect. It was not.

The four scripts are not uniform in mechanism, which matters for any future fix:

- **C1** (`integrate_b4_ci_c1.py:296`) compares a SHA256 of the current excluded rows
  against a static literal, `EXCLUDED_BASE_ROWS_SHA256`. This is mechanically recomputable.
- **C2/C3/C4** (each via a `base_cells()` helper) instead compare the current local rows
  directly against `git show <BASE_COMMIT>:.../cells.tsv` — a specific historical commit,
  not a literal. There is no hash to recompute.

## Why This Was Not Fixed In That Lane

Deciding whether the Arc 4b split is an approved base change for the B4-CI C1–C4 closures is
an owner decision, not a mechanical one. That context does not belong to the Arc 5/6
census-count merge this finding surfaced during. Fixing it by recomputing hashes or advancing
`BASE_COMMIT` without that context would risk exactly the kind of laundering these guards
exist to prevent.

## Disposition at the time

- `test_b3_q6_target_promotion.py` (the fifth orphaned file, and the one directly exercised
  by Arc 5/6's promotions) was fixed and wired into `.github/workflows/R-CMD-check.yaml` as
  an explicit per-file invocation. CI went from 2 of 7 `tools/tests/` files run to 3 of 7.
- `test_b4_ci_c1.py` … `c4.py` remained un-wired and were **not** skipped or xfailed — the
  workflow carries a comment naming them and this note, so the gap stays visible rather than
  silent. `unittest discover` was deliberately not adopted as the replacement mechanism: it
  would silently re-include all four the moment someone made that swap for an unrelated
  reason.

# Part 2 — The resolution (`claude/b4-ci-mc0207-rebaseline`)

## Goal

Make all four scripts and their tests pass against current `main` and wire them into CI —
without weakening what they check.

## What was actually drifting

Part 1 named `mc-0207`. A full per-script diff found **four** drifted cells, from **three**
separately reviewed arcs. Re-baselining on `mc-0207` alone would have left three still red.

| cell | change | arc | affects |
|---|---|---|---|
| `mc-0207` | `point_fit_recovery → none`, plus `route_variant`/`q_gate`/`work_status`/`tranche_id` | Arc 4b q4/q6/q8 split (#905) | all four |
| `mc-0269` | `point_fit_recovery → interval_feasible` | Arc 1 REML-slope campaign | all four |
| `mc-0199`, `mc-0672` | `interval_feasible → inference_ready_with_caveats` | spatial-q2 confidence-eye (#903) | C3, C4 |

C3's failure is also a *different class* from C2's and C4's: not a protected-row drift but a
**cohort-own** drift, because `mc-0199` and `mc-0672` are C3's own cells that a later arc
promoted further. Cohort evidence and transition records were 100% intact in all four.

Decisively: **every one of the four has a transition row accounting for it**, and each row's
current `primary_evidence_id` matches that transition's evidence. That is what made a
provenance rule available rather than hypothetical.

## Implemented

`tools/b4_ci_guard.py` (new) replaces the neighbour check in all four scripts.

The claim that matters — *cohort Cn's `--apply` did not touch its neighbours* — is true
forever. Byte-equality against `BASE_COMMIT` asserts the strictly stronger *no LATER arc
touched them either*, which no reviewer agreed to and which decays the moment any approved
arc lands. The replacement is two checks that together are **stronger**:

1. **Identity is frozen forever.** `cell_id`, `source_order`, `axis`, `family_route`,
   `family_type`, `model_type`, `dpar`, `effect_type`, `structure_provider`, `dimension`,
   `estimator` may never change for a neighbour.
2. **Evidence-bearing fields may move, but only with recorded provenance** — a transition in
   the append-only ledger, belonging to some *other* cohort, naming the evidence the row's
   `primary_evidence_id` now cites.

An unexplained edit still fails, a deleted row still fails, and a cohort quietly claiming a
neighbour now fails **by name** (`this cohort claims it via ev-…`) instead of as an opaque
`base-row drift`. `apply()` keeps the strict byte-equality pre-flight on purpose:
re-applying a stale cohort over newer state *should* refuse.

This answers Part 1's open question — *should `BASE_COMMIT` advance, or should `mc-0207`
leave `EXCLUDED_IDS`?* — with **neither**. `BASE_COMMIT` stays frozen as provenance and
`mc-0207` stays excluded; what changes is that "unchanged" is now expressed as accounted-for
rather than as equality against a snapshot.

## Files Changed

- `tools/b4_ci_guard.py` — new: `IDENTITY_FIELDS`, `identity_digest`, `unexplained_drift`,
  `unaccounted_provenance`, `describe`
- `tools/integrate_b4_ci_c1.py` — `EXCLUDED_BASE_ROWS_SHA256` (full-row) →
  `EXCLUDED_IDENTITY_SHA256` (identity projection) + a local-only provenance check,
  preserving c1's deliberate no-git-history property
- `tools/integrate_b4_ci_c2.py`, `c3.py`, `c4.py` — neighbour check → `unexplained_drift`;
  `c3`/`c4` also apply it to their cohort-own rows
- `tools/integrate_b4_ci_c4.py` — gains `--check-with-later-cohorts`, matching c2/c3
- `tools/tests/test_b4_ci_c4.py` — moves to that mode
- `tools/tests/test_b4_ci_guard.py` — new, 12 tests pinning the guard's semantics

## Checks Run

```
python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c1.py --check-current              # OK
python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c2.py --check-with-later-cohorts   # OK
python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c3.py --check-with-later-cohorts   # OK
python3 tools/integrate_b4_ci_c4.py --check-with-later-cohorts   # OK
python3 -m unittest tools.tests.test_b4_ci_guard                 # 12 OK
python3 -m unittest tools.tests.test_b4_ci_c{1,2,3,4}            # 14 OK
```

The identity digest of the four excluded rows is **byte-identical at `BASE_COMMIT`
`7c3bc8b3f` and at `HEAD`** (`73b541a0a943a528…`) — direct evidence that only
evidence-bearing fields moved, never identity.

## Tests Of The Tests

Each pre-existing negative control was re-checked against the new guard rather than assumed:

- tampering an excluded neighbour's `source_order` (the existing
  `test_rejects_b3_and_exclusion_breaches` mutation) still fails, now reporting
  `identity fields changed: source_order`
- a tier move with no accounting transition fails
- an identity change fails **even when** a transition would otherwise account for the row
- a cohort naming its own evidence on a neighbour fails as an unauthorised claim
- a cohort's own transition cannot self-account for a neighbour
- a deleted row fails; a row created after the freeze is correctly ignored

## Known Limitations

`c2`/`c3`/`c4` read their `BASE_COMMIT` through `git show <sha>:…`, and
`.github/workflows/R-CMD-check.yaml` uses `actions/checkout@v6.0.2` with no `fetch-depth`
— a **shallow clone**. They therefore need their base commits present before they can run in
CI. That is presumably why `c1`'s `check_current()` was written "without source-history
access" in the first place.

`fetch-depth: 0` is the wrong remedy: `.git` is **691 MB** across 2062 commits, and paying
that on every pull request contradicts the standing rule to keep Actions cheap. The fix is a
targeted shallow fetch of just the four base commits — all four are ancestors of `main`,
hence reachable and fetchable by SHA — with the SHAs read out of the scripts so they cannot
drift.

`apply()` in all four is now effectively un-runnable against current `main`, by design.

## Team Learning

The B4-CI guards and the `mc-0424` reconciler defect are the **same failure at two levels**:
a check exists, no runner invokes it, and the evidence record still credits it
(`reviewed_by: tools/arc2_profile_reconcile.py`). The generalisable rule: **a guard's
definition of done includes the line in CI that runs it.**

Second: a frozen baseline is a claim about *time*, and time-coupled invariants rot silently.
Where the real invariant is "I didn't do this", express it as provenance, not as equality
against a snapshot.
