## 11. Team Learning

- Read the engine's answer, not the gate's: the `engine-direct` layer is what
  turns "the bridge refused" into "the ENGINE refused, with this message".
- A parity comparator must compare the parameter the shape exists for. For a
  random slope that is the SD/cor block, and an empty accessor is a reporting
  defect to name, not a reason to skip the comparison.
- A digest that covers the namespace cannot be quoted inside the namespace.
