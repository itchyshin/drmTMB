#### What to look for ####
Regime 1: all three engines should agree closely, and MSPL should shrink
  only mildly -- the penalty must not disturb an identified fit.
Regime 2: ML and glmer both diverge on the separated coefficient AND
  DISAGREE WITH EACH OTHER, because they stop at different points on an
  almost-flat likelihood. That disagreement is the tell. MSPL stays finite
  and its three identified coefficients still match ML to ~2 decimals.
